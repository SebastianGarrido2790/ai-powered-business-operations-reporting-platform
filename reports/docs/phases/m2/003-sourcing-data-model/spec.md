# 003 — Sourcing Data Model

**Phase:** M2 — Data Integration & Processing  
**Status:** Approved — feature specification and feature plan/tasks approved by the project owner 2026-11-14; no implementation authorized  
**Parent decision record:** `../implementation_plan.md`  
**Governing decisions:** M2-D03, M2-D03.1; M2-D04 and M2-D05 for persisted outcomes and normalized values; M2-D06 for relationship and authority columns; M2-D07 for read access  
**Execution stage:** Stage 2 per §7.2; precedes SDD 002 per the confirmed sequencing resolution

## 1. Purpose, context, and expected outcome

**Purpose**

Create the `sourcing` schema, its raw/staging and curated persistence layers, provenance, and identity rules so that ingestion can capture, validate, normalize, reconcile, and retrieve sourcing data without losing the source of truth.

**Context and problem**

The `sourcing` schema does not exist. M1 provides only the `platform` schema and durable job execution. SDD 001 produces a source-neutral fetch envelope; SDD 002 turns an ingestion request into a job and a run; SDDs 004 and 005 add validation, normalization, and reconciliation. None of them can persist or retrieve anything until the Sourcing schema, the record identity rules, and the persistence ports exist. The approved M2 decisions fix three logical layers — raw capture, normalized records, and curated current state — and require traceable provenance.

**Expected outcome**

A reviewed migration creates the `sourcing` schema and eight tables. Every imported record is retained as received, can carry a normalized representation, and is traceable to its source, run, and platform job. Repeated ingestion appends source records and upserts curated entities without duplication. SDDs 002, 004, 005, and 006 consume typed persistence ports rather than SQL.

**Requirements addressed**

FR-ING-05, FR-DAT-06, MVP-04, NFR-DAT-01.

## 2. Scope

**Included**

- The `sourcing` schema and two module-owned Alembic revisions.
- `sourcing.ingestion_runs`, `sourcing.source_records`, and `sourcing.validation_issues`.
- `sourcing.suppliers`, `sourcing.requisitions`, `sourcing.quotes`, `sourcing.purchase_orders`, and `sourcing.deliveries`.
- Provenance columns, record identity, and idempotent upsert rules.
- `SourcingRunStore`, `SourcingRecordStore`, and `SourcingEntityStore` ports and concrete stores.
- A raw-capture pipeline that persists source records for SDD 002's handler.
- Module-owned ORM models that mirror the migrations.
- PostgreSQL-gated migration, constraint, provenance, and idempotency tests.

**Excluded**

- Validation rules, normalization algorithms, and quarantine decisions; owned by SDD 004.
- Duplicate detection, matching cascade, source authority, and link confirmation; owned by SDD 005.
- API endpoints, pagination, filtering, and response schemas; owned by SDD 006.
- `analytics_sourcing`, materialized views, Power BI, KPIs, and reporting.
- Supplier messages, exceptions, alerts, approvals, and their tables.
- Retention, deletion, tombstone propagation, and archival.
- New database roles, grants, or schema-level permission changes.
- Changes to the `platform` schema, M1 job tables, or the module manifest.
- Startup DDL, `create_all()`, and autogenerated migrations.

## 3. Required behavior

1. Applying the module revisions to an empty database shall create the `sourcing` schema and all eight tables; downgrading shall remove them in dependency order without cascading destructive drops.
2. `sourcing.ingestion_runs` shall store one row per execution with the platform job, source, requested entity types, principal-scoped idempotency key, correlation identifier, content hash or acquisition reference, terminal outcome, counts, run-level warning codes, timestamps, and a sanitized error summary.
3. `sourcing.source_records` shall store one row per source record per run with the raw payload, the normalized payload when available, the external identifier when provided, a deterministic record key, the content fingerprint when SDD 005 has computed it, the source timestamp, the validation status, the normalization version, and provenance metadata.
4. `sourcing.validation_issues` shall store one row per issue with the run, the source record, a stable rule code, an issue type, a severity, the affected field, a sanitized message, a sanitized value excerpt, and a deterministic issue key.
5. The five curated tables shall store the normalized business fields and provenance for suppliers, requisitions, quotes, purchase orders, and deliveries, using an internal UUID primary key and a unique source-qualified external identity where one exists.
6. The raw, normalized, and curated representations shall remain distinguishable; raw payloads shall be retained after normalization and promotion.
7. Record identity shall be deterministic. Re-running the same job shall upsert source records and issues by run-scoped keys and shall not duplicate them.
8. Curated upserts shall be idempotent. The first ingestion run shall be immutable; the last run, latest source record, source timestamp, and processed timestamp shall update; historical observations shall remain in `source_records`.
9. A repeated external identifier with different content within a run shall produce distinct record keys and a duplicate or conflict issue; the platform shall not silently choose a winner.
10. The run outcome shall be terminal-only. Counts shall be non-negative. The run shall have no status column and shall not implement a second job state machine.
11. Relationship foreign keys shall be nullable so unresolved and ambiguous links remain representable; the schema shall not invent a link.
12. Provenance shall be queryable from a curated row to its latest source record, ingestion run, and platform job.
13. The persistence ports shall expose the operations required by SDDs 002, 004, 005, and 006. Unknown entity types shall be rejected, and no entity type shall be interpolated into SQL.
14. No `analytics_sourcing` schema, materialized view, retention or deletion process, tombstone, startup DDL, `create_all()`, new database role, or new dependency shall be introduced.
15. Errors, logs, issue values, and persisted payloads shall be sanitized and bounded; the platform shall not persist resolved secrets or credentials.

- `UNIQUE (job_id)` — one run per platform job.
- `UNIQUE (submitted_by, idempotency_key)` — principal-scoped replay.
- `outcome IS NULL OR outcome IN ('SUCCEEDED', 'FAILED')`.
- `(outcome IS NULL) = (completed_at IS NULL)` — terminal outcome and timestamp agree.
- `cardinality(entity_types) > 0` and `entity_types <@ ARRAY['supplier', 'requisition', 'quote', 'purchase_order', 'delivery']::text[]`.
- `content_hash IS NULL OR content_hash ~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$'`.
- `NOT ('' = ANY(warnings))`.
- All six counts `>= 0`.
- `completed_at IS NULL OR started_at IS NULL OR completed_at >= started_at`.
- Index `(source_id, created_at DESC)` for run listing.
- Partial index `(source_id, content_hash) WHERE content_hash IS NOT NULL` for duplicate-content lookup.

Counts are non-negative integers. The intended full-pipeline relationships are `received = valid + quarantined`, `warning <= valid`, `duplicate <= received`, and `unresolved <= valid`; the database enforces only non-negativity because the raw-capture stage completes a run before validation and reconciliation populate the other counts.

`warnings` holds sanitized run-level codes that have no source record to attach to, such as `DUPLICATE_CONTENT` for identical file content submitted under a new key. Record-level findings belong in `validation_issues`, not here. SDD 002 sets the submission-level warning when it creates the run; SDD 002 decides whether the status response exposes the codes.

## 5. Record identity and idempotent persistence

### 5.1 Curated identity

- Every curated row uses an internal UUID primary key.
- The origin identity is `(origin_source_id, origin_external_record_id)`, is set at creation, and is unique where the external identifier exists.
- A record that requires an identity but lacks one is quarantined as a data-quality issue; no business identifier is invented.
- A repeated external identifier within one run with different content produces distinct record keys, so both observations remain visible and SDD 005 can raise a duplicate or conflict issue.

### 5.2 Record key

`record_key` is deterministic and bounded:

```text
record_key = "rec:" + sha256(
    entity_type + "\x00" + (external_record_id or "") + "\x00" + canonical_json(raw_payload)
).hexdigest() + ":" + occurrence
```

- `canonical_json` sorts object keys, uses compact separators, and encodes UTF-8.
- `occurrence` is the zero-based count of earlier records in the same run with the same digest, so identical records remain distinct.
- The key is stable for a stable source snapshot and deterministic connector ordering. Distinct records may be reordered without changing their keys.

### 5.3 Upsert semantics

- Within a run, `source_records` is upserted by `(ingestion_run_id, record_key)`. A retry does not create a second row for the same record key.
- Across runs, every run appends its own source records; historical observations are never deleted or rewritten.
- `validation_issues` is upserted by `(source_record_id, issue_key)`, so a retry does not duplicate issues.
- Curated entities are upserted by a resolved entity identifier or by origin identity. Repeated calls are idempotent.
- On a curated update, `first_ingestion_run_id` is unchanged; `last_ingestion_run_id`, `latest_source_record_id`, `source_timestamp` (when provided), `processed_at`, and `updated_at` are refreshed.
- M2 ingestion is additive/upsert. Absence from a source snapshot does not delete or deactivate a curated record.

## 6. Provenance and traceability

- Every source record carries its run, source, external identifier, source timestamp, received timestamp, connector type, source revision, and optional acquisition reference and content hash.
- Every curated row points to its origin identity, first and last run, and latest source record.
- The trace path is: curated row → `latest_source_record_id` → `ingestion_run_id` → `job_id` → `platform.jobs`.
- Validation status is persisted on the source record and on the curated row. Reconciliation status is persisted on the curated row.
- The normalization version is persisted on the source record so reprocessing can be reproduced.
- Raw and normalized payloads remain distinguishable; promotion does not remove the raw payload.
- Issue rows carry a stable rule code and sanitized value excerpt, so an investigation can explain an outcome without reading unrestricted payloads.
- Attempt history remains in `platform.job_attempts`; the run stores no attempt rows and does not duplicate the job state machine.
- A `SUCCEEDED` or `FAILED` platform job with a null run outcome, or a non-terminal job with a terminal outcome, is a detectable inconsistency; the status projection compares the job status and the run outcome. `NEEDS_REVIEW` is an interrupted run, not a contradiction: the run keeps a null outcome and the projection reports `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

## 7. Persistence ports

The concrete stores live in `src/app/modules/sourcing/persistence/`. Ports are typed and transport-neutral; they do not expose ORM models to callers.

### 7.1 `SourcingRunStore`

| Operation | Semantics |
|---|---|
| `create_run(session, run)` | Insert and flush using the caller's session, including any run-level warning codes; do not commit. A duplicate principal/key surfaces as an integrity error. |
| `get_run(ingestion_id)` | Return the run or `None`. |
| `get_run_by_job_id(job_id)` | Return the linked run or `None`. |
| `get_run_by_idempotency_key(submitted_by, idempotency_key)` | Return the principal-scoped run or `None`. |
| `find_by_content_hash(source_id, content_hash)` | Return the most recent matching run regardless of outcome, or `None`. |
| `mark_running(ingestion_id, started_at)` | Set `started_at` only if null; refresh `updated_at`; reject a terminal run. |
| `record_attempt_error(ingestion_id, error_summary)` | Store the latest sanitized summary while in progress; no-op once terminal. |
| `complete_run(ingestion_id, outcome, counts, completed_at, error_summary, warnings=())` | Set the terminal outcome, counts, timestamp, sanitized summary, and any additional run-level warning codes. Same-outcome completion is idempotent; a contradictory outcome raises a state conflict. |

`complete_run` maps the count keys `received`, `valid`, `quarantined`, `warning`, `duplicate`, and `unresolved` to the six count columns, rejects unknown or negative values, and treats omitted keys as zero. The caller passes the complete aggregate. Warning codes are unioned with the codes already stored, so a submission-level `DUPLICATE_CONTENT` warning survives completion; blank codes are rejected.

### 7.2 `SourcingRecordStore`

| Operation | Semantics |
|---|---|
| `append_source_record(session, record)` | Upsert by `(ingestion_run_id, record_key)`; preserve `received_at`, normalized payload, and validation status for an identical re-capture. |
| `set_normalized(record_id, normalized_payload, validation_status, normalization_version)` | Persist the normalized representation and the resulting status; reject `PENDING`. |
| `set_content_fingerprint(record_id, fingerprint, *, session=None)` | Persist the content fingerprint for a promoted record; never commits a caller session. |
| `find_by_content_fingerprint(source_id, fingerprint, *, exclude_source_record_id, limit, session=None)` | Bounded, ordered `RecordReference` lookup for SDD 005's potential-duplicate detection. |
| `find_run_records_by_source_identity(ingestion_run_id, source_id, external_record_id, *, exclude_source_record_id, limit, session=None)` | Bounded, ordered `RecordReference` lookup for SDD 005's exact-duplicate and identity/content detection. |
| `add_issue(session, issue)` | Upsert by `(source_record_id, issue_key)`; preserve the original creation timestamp. |
| `list_source_records(ingestion_run_id, limit, offset)` | Bounded, stable-ordered read. Not consumed by SDD 006, which exposes no source-record endpoint. |
| `list_issues(ingestion_run_id, *, issue_type, severity, rule_code, field_name, entity_type, limit, offset)` | Bounded, stable-ordered `IssueView` read for SDD 006; optional filters compose with AND (additive SDD 006 amendment). |

`IssueView` projects the issue columns plus `record_key` and `entity_type` from the issuing source record; it never loads a raw or normalized payload. SDD 006 owns the filter parameters and the page defaults.

### 7.3 `SourcingEntityStore`

| Operation | Semantics |
|---|---|
| `upsert_entity(session, entity_type, identity, values, provenance, update_mode)` | Insert or update a curated row; reject an unknown entity type. |
| `get_entity(entity_type, entity_id)` | Return the curated row or `None`. |
| `find_by_source_identity(entity_type, source_id, external_record_id, *, session=None)` | Return the row matching the origin identity or `None`. |
| `find_candidates(entity_type, business_identifier, normalized_name_key, limit, *, session=None)` | Return bounded candidates matching the supplied deterministic keys for SDD 005's cascade. |

`identity` is either a resolved entity identifier or a source identity. `update_mode` is `REPLACE` for an authoritative source or `FILL_MISSING` for a non-authoritative source; both modes update provenance, while `REPLACE` overwrites only the business fields supplied and `FILL_MISSING` writes only fields that are currently null. SDD 005 chooses the mode from source authority and owns the matching cascade; `find_candidates` only applies the filters it is given.

### 7.4 Raw-capture pipeline

The feature provides a raw-capture implementation consumed by SDD 002's handler:

```python
class RawCapturePipeline:
    def process(self, *, run, fetch_result, budget) -> CaptureResult: ...


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class CaptureResult:
    counts: Mapping[str, int]
    warnings: tuple[str, ...] = ()
```

- `run` is the ingestion run record; `fetch_result` is SDD 001's source-neutral envelope; `budget` exposes `check(records_processed=0)`, which raises when the deadline passes.
- `counts` uses the same keys as the run counts; `warnings` carries stable, sanitized codes.
- For each record, the pipeline validates the entity type, computes the record key, enforces the raw-payload bound, and appends the source record with `validation_status = PENDING`.
- A record exceeding the raw-payload bound is persisted with a bounded, explicitly truncated payload, marked `QUARANTINED`, and given a capture-level issue with a stable code; it is not silently dropped.
- Records are persisted in short transactions per fetch page. A transient persistence failure rolls back the page and propagates to the handler; the store does not retry internally.
- The result reports at least `received` and `quarantined`; validation, warning, duplicate, and unresolved counts remain zero until SDDs 004 and 005 populate them. SDD 002 may pass `warnings` to `complete_run` for persistence.
- The pipeline performs no validation rules, normalization, deduplication, reconciliation, or curated upserts.

### 7.5 Transaction and error boundaries

- `create_run` and `upsert_entity` use the caller's session; the caller owns the commit and rollback.
- Reads and terminal updates open short store-owned sessions unless a caller session is supplied.
- The handler executes outside a database transaction; the pipeline opens one transaction per page or batch.
- Missing runs raise a not-found error; contradictory terminal writes raise a state-conflict error; duplicate principal/key surfaces as an integrity error for SDD 002 to resolve as a replay.
- Store errors include identifiers and safe messages only; they never include raw payloads, secrets, or stack traces.

## 8. Migration and ownership rules

- The `sourcing` schema and tables are created by hand-written, module-owned revisions in the existing single Alembic history: `0003_sourcing_ingestion` and `0004_sourcing_curated_entities`.
- `0003` creates the schema and the ingestion, raw-capture, and issue tables. `0004` creates the five curated tables.
- `0004.down_revision` is `0003`; `0003.down_revision` is `0002_platform_jobs_and_metadata`.
- Downgrades drop tables in reverse dependency order and then drop the schema without `CASCADE`; leftover objects fail loudly.
- `target_metadata` remains `None`; migrations are not autogenerated from module models. Module models mirror reviewed migrations for typed access and never create schema.
- No startup DDL and no `create_all()`.
- No `analytics_sourcing` schema, materialized view, or reporting table.
- The existing application database role owns the schema in M2. Separate schema grants remain a deployment-hardening item and must be resolved before production reliance.

## 9. Error handling

- A missing run, source record, or entity raises a not-found error.
- A contradictory terminal outcome raises a state-conflict error and leaves the row unchanged.
- A duplicate principal/key surfaces as an integrity error; SDD 002 resolves it as a replay or reports an inconsistency.
- An unknown entity type raises a validation error; it is never interpolated into SQL.
- A raw payload over the bound produces a record-level issue, not a job failure.
- A transient persistence failure propagates as a transient error; the handler applies the M1 retry policy.
- Migration failures are operator-visible and never trigger automatic repair or schema mutation at startup.
- Errors and logs include ingestion and job identifiers, source identifier, and a safe message; they never include secrets, credentials, full connection strings, unrestricted payloads, or stack traces.

## 10. Non-functional requirements

- **Security:** closed entity-type set; no dynamic SQL; raw payloads and issue values are untrusted and sanitized; no resolved secret is persisted; the existing database role is used without new grants.
- **Reliability:** foreign keys use `ON DELETE RESTRICT`; no cascades; transactional pages; idempotent upserts; migrations are versioned and operator-invoked.
- **Observability:** run, job, source, correlation, counts, timestamps, rule codes, and validation outcomes are queryable.
- **Performance:** indexes cover run lookup, content-hash lookup, source-record listing, issue filtering, and curated identity; list operations are bounded.
- **Determinism:** record keys and issue keys are deterministic; identical inputs produce identical persistence effects.
- **Compatibility:** PostgreSQL 16; single Alembic history; no new infrastructure, broker, object store, or analytics schema.
- **Maintainability:** module-owned schema, models, migrations, and stores; Platform Core is unchanged; ports are typed and explicit.

## 14. Dependencies and sequencing

- **SDD 001** — source identity, entity types, external identifiers, content hash, and opaque acquisition reference.
- **SDD 002** — consumes `SourcingRunStore` for atomic run creation, lookup, lifecycle updates, and completion; consumes the raw-capture pipeline through its handler.
- **SDD 004** — consumes `source_records` and `validation_issues`, sets normalized payloads and statuses, and extends the pipeline.
- **SDD 005** — consumes the record and entity stores, sets reconciliation status and links, chooses the update mode from source authority, and writes content fingerprints through the additive SDD 005 amendment.
- **SDD 006** — consumes the run, record, and entity read operations for listing, issues, and entity retrieval, and extends `list_issues` with its filters and the `IssueView` projection through the additive SDD 006 amendment.
- **M1** — provides `platform.jobs` and `platform.job_attempts`; the run references the job and does not duplicate attempt history.

**Sequencing resolution (confirmed 2026-11-14)**

SDD 003 precedes SDD 002, consistent with §7.2. This feature owns the schema, the run-store port, and the raw-capture entry point.

**Raw-capture interface reconciliation**

Because 003 precedes 002, 003 does not import SDD 002's `IngestionPipeline` or `ExecutionBudget`. The raw-capture pipeline exposes `process(*, run, fetch_result, budget) -> CaptureResult` and calls `budget.check(...)` structurally. SDD 002's handler adapts `CaptureResult` to its own `ProcessingResult` where a nominal type is required. This preserves 002's intent that 003 provides raw capture without creating a reverse dependency. It is an interface refinement, not a scope change.
